## How to study

**Guided practice** builds understanding using structured explanations and exercises. **Exploratory practice** provides less guidance and requires you to determine the next investigative steps yourself. Mix both: learn the tool, reproduce examples, then attempt an unfamiliar target and reflect on your process.

Keep a record of scope, commands, output, mistakes and verification. Avoid confusing reading a walkthrough with personally completing an exercise.
